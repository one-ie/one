# Thai Visa Guide

Let me help you navigate Thailand's visa system with detailed information and requirements.

## 1. Tourist Visa (TR)

### Types Available
| Visa Type | Duration | Entries | Fee |
|-----------|----------|---------|-----|
| Tourist Single Entry | 60 days | 1 | 1,000 THB |
| Tourist Multiple Entry | 6 months | Multiple | 5,000 THB |
| Visa on Arrival | 15 days | 1 | 2,000 THB |

### Required Documents
1. **Basic Requirements**
   - Valid passport (6+ months)
   - Completed application form
   - Recent photos (4x6cm)
   - Flight bookings
   - Accommodation proof

2. **Financial Evidence**
   ```markdown
   Personal: 20,000 THB per person
   Family: 40,000 THB per family
   ```

## 2. Non-Immigrant Visa Types

### Business Visa (Non-B)
> **Key Requirements**
> - Company registration documents
> - Work permit application
> - Employment contract
> - Educational credentials

### Education Visa (Non-ED)
```markdown
Required Items:
- School acceptance letter
- Course registration
- Ministry of Education approval
- Financial statements
```

### Retirement Visa (Non-O-A)
**Financial Requirements**
- 800,000 THB in Thai bank OR
- Monthly income of 65,000 THB OR
- Combination of both

### Marriage Visa (Non-O)
**Documentation Needed**
- Marriage certificate
- Spouse's ID/passport
- Financial proof (400,000 THB)
- Photos together
- House registration

## 3. Application Process

### Step-by-Step Guide
1. **Preparation**
   - Gather documents
   - Complete forms
   - Prepare finances

2. **Submission**
   - Book appointment
   - Submit application
   - Pay fees

3. **Processing**
   - Wait period (2-5 days)
   - Track application
   - Collect passport

## 4. Important Tips

### Do's and Don'ts
✅ **Recommended**
- Apply early
- Double-check documents
- Keep copies
- Follow up regularly

❌ **Avoid**
- Incomplete applications
- Expired documents
- Working without permit
- Overstaying visa

## 5. Common Questions

> **Q: Can I extend my Tourist Visa?**  
> A: Yes, 30-day extensions available at immigration offices for 1,900 THB

> **Q: When should I do 90-day reporting?**  
> A: Within 15 days before or 7 days after your due date

> **Q: Can I convert my Tourist Visa?**  
> A: Generally no, must exit and apply for new visa type
