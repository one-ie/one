**Module 0: FOUNDATION – Building Your Strategic Bedrock**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Stop guessing, start knowing. Build the essential foundation for targeted AI generation and strategic decision-making.
*   **Description:** Before taking action, forge the bedrock for success. Deeply understand **Your Ideal Customer** (their pains, desires, context), define **Your Company Context** (unique offer, brand voice), and assess **Market Awareness** (competition, trends).
*   **What You'll Create:** Your comprehensive **Strategic Blueprint** (Customer + Company + Market Profiles) ready to fuel hyper-effective AI prompts.
*   *(Lesson examples remain the same: Ideal Customer Avatar, Company Context, Market Awareness, Alignment Principle)*

**Module 1: HOOK (Step 1) – Locating Prospects & Initiating Engagement**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Strategically identify where your ideal customers are, understand their awareness level, and craft compelling initial messages ('hooks') to capture relevant attention across key channels.
*   **Description:** Go beyond basic hooks. Learn to proactively **find** prospects by identifying their "watering holes" and search intent. Master selecting the right channels and leverage the AI Playbook to craft tailored, attention-grabbing **initial engagement messages** (ad headlines, content titles, social openers) suitable for different awareness stages.
*   **What You'll Create:** A prioritized channel strategy and a toolkit of AI-generated, context-aware **'FIND' assets** (hooks) ready for deployment.
*   **Lessons:** Prospect Awareness Stages, Target Location & Intent (Watering Holes/Search), Hook Crafting & Channel Strategy.

**Module 2: GIFT (Step 2) – Delivering Instant Value & Building Interest**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Convert initial attention into genuine interest with a high-value, problem-solving lead magnet.
*   **Description:** Move beyond attention to genuine engagement. Use AI prompts to rapidly brainstorm, outline, and draft content for **high-value Lead Magnets (Gifts)** your found prospects actually want. Craft compelling landing page copy and promotional messages that motivate immediate action.
*   **What You'll Create:** A ready-to-deploy lead magnet, high-converting landing page copy drafts, and supporting promotional copy.
*   *(Lesson examples remain similar: Lead Magnet Creation, Landing Page Copy, Promotional Strategy)*

**Module 3: IDENTIFY (Step 3) – Seamlessly Capturing Your Future Customers**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Convert interested prospects (who want the Gift) into known, contactable leads efficiently and ethically.
*   **Description:** Turn clicks into contacts smoothly. Master the **low-friction ask**. Use AI-assisted prompts to optimize opt-in forms/messaging. Set up basic tagging and initial welcome/delivery mechanisms for proper lead flow into your systems.
*   **What You'll Create:** An optimized lead capture process, configured initial tagging, and welcome/delivery email drafts.
*   *(Lesson examples remain similar: Form Optimization, Welcome Sequence/Delivery, Lead Tagging & System Integration)*

**Module 4: ENGAGE (Step 4) – Reducing Friction & Closing the Sale**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Interact effectively during the critical moments of the buying process to address final hesitations, reduce friction, and maximize completed purchases.
*   **Description:** Don't lose sales at the finish line! Learn strategies to **engage prospects during the checkout or decision-making phase**. Use AI prompts to draft helpful FAQ responses, concise chatbot snippets for common queries, or trust-building messages that provide reassurance and guide users towards completing the sale.
*   **What You'll Create:** AI-assisted assets for real-time support (chatbot scripts, FAQ answers) and friction-reduction messages for checkout.
*   **Lessons:** Identifying Purchase Friction Points, Crafting Real-Time Support Assets (AI-Assisted), Checkout Confidence Builders.

**Module 5: SELL (Step 5) – Engineering Your High-Conversion Sales Environment**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Transform your product/sales pages into powerful conversion engines by optimizing the core offer presentation.
*   **Description:** Optimize the crucial point of sale. Leverage AI prompts to craft **persuasive sales headlines, benefit-driven descriptions, objection-handling copy, and optimized CTAs**. Integrate social proof effectively and apply conversion design principles (informed by Foundation & Hormozi's Value Equation) to maximize purchase decisions from qualified visitors.
*   **What You'll Create:** High-converting sales page/product description copy drafts and a blueprint for an optimized sales environment.
*   *(Lesson examples remain similar: Sales Page Copywriting (AI-Assisted), Objection Handling & Proof Integration, Call-to-Action Optimization)*

**Module 6: NURTURE (Step 6) – Systematically Converting the "Not Yet Ready"**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Build trust, deliver value, and convert prospects over time, while strategically re-engaging non-buyers through targeted campaigns.
*   **Description:** Realize most sales happen *after* the first interaction. Master follow-up with **AI-powered nurturing**. Use the Playbook to rapidly draft entire **value-driven email sequences** that build relationships, shift beliefs, and strategically introduce your offer. Simultaneously, craft targeted **retargeting ad campaigns** based on user behavior.
*   **What You'll Create:** Automated nurture email sequence drafts (value, pre-frame, offer) and targeted retargeting ad copy drafts.
*   *(Lesson examples remain similar: Email Sequence Strategy & Drafting (AI-Assisted), Retargeting Campaign Setup & Copy, Content Strategy for Nurturing)*

**Module 7: UPSELL (Step 7) – Instantly Maximizing Profit Per Customer**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Increase Average Order Value (AOV) and customer profitability immediately post-purchase.
*   **Description:** Unlock immediate additional revenue. Discover how to use AI prompts to brainstorm and craft compelling **post-purchase upsell and cross-sell offers**. Learn where and when to present these logically for maximum take-rate, boosting the profitability of each acquisition.
*   **What You'll Create:** High-converting upsell offer concepts and AI-generated promotional copy drafts.
*   *(Lesson examples remain similar: Upsell Offer Creation & Framing, Timing & Placement Strategy, AOV Optimization Tactics)*

**Module 8: UNDERSTAND (Step 8) – Driving Loyalty Through Onboarding & Insight**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Maximize customer success through effective onboarding and gather actionable data for retention and ongoing optimization.
*   **Description:** Turn buyers into loyal fans and raving advocates. Craft exceptional **customer onboarding sequences** using AI prompts to ensure quick success and validate their purchase decision. Learn methods to **gather and potentially analyze customer feedback** (using AI for summarization/themes) for deep insights, enabling proactive communication and reducing churn.
*   **What You'll Create:** Effective onboarding email sequence drafts and a strategy for leveraging customer feedback.
*   *(Lesson examples remain similar: Onboarding Flow Design (AI-Assisted), Feedback Collection & Analysis Strategies, Customer Retention Tactics)*

**Module 9: SHARE (Step 9) – Amplifying Growth Through Advocacy**
*(~3 Lessons)*
*   **Objective:** Systematically leverage happy customers to drive exponential growth via social proof and referrals.
*   **Description:** Transform satisfied customers into your most powerful marketing engine. Use AI prompts to streamline requests for **reviews and testimonials**. Learn to effortlessly turn glowing feedback into compelling social proof assets (posts, ad snippets). Build systematic **referral programs**, amplifying reach and credibility through genuine customer advocacy.
*   **What You'll Create:** Templates for review requests (AI-assisted), strategies for deploying social proof, and referral campaign framework ideas.
*   *(Lesson examples remain similar: Review Generation & Management, Social Proof Amplification, Referral System Design)*
