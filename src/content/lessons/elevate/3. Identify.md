### **Module 3: IDENTIFY (Step 3) – Seamlessly Capturing Your Future Customers**

---

#### **1. Module Introduction**
*   **Format:** Text

Welcome to Module 3, the crucial final step in the **ATTRACT** level: **IDENTIFY**. In Module 2 (GIFT), you designed and offered a high-value lead magnet that successfully captured the *interest* of prospects found via your HOOK (Module 1). Now, we need to convert that interest into a tangible asset: a known lead in your system.

The **objective of the IDENTIFY step** is to smoothly and efficiently capture essential contact information (primarily email) from prospects who want your Gift, transforming them from anonymous visitors into identifiable contacts you can build a relationship with through the CONVERT and GROW stages.

This step is the bridge between attraction and conversion. We'll focus on optimizing the data capture mechanism, crafting clear 'ask' copy, ensuring data flows correctly, and setting up basic tracking – all while maintaining the trust you've started building. Successfully identifying leads here allows you to move into **Module 4: ENGAGE** with a list of qualified prospects ready for the next interaction.

#### **4. Actionable Task Checklist**
*   **Format:** Text List, Downloadable Sheet

*   [ ] **Review GIFT Offer:** Confirm the specific Gift being offered and the page/method where the opt-in will occur.
*   [ ] **Choose Capture Mechanism:** Decide: Inline form on landing page? Popup? Chatbot interaction?
*   [ ] **Define Required Information:** Determine the *absolute minimum* fields needed (likely just email).
*   [ ] **Draft "Ask" Copy (Use AI Prompt ID1):** Generate variations for requesting the information clearly and concisely. *Refine.*
*   [ ] **Draft Welcome/Delivery Email (Use AI Prompt ID2):** Prepare the immediate follow-up email delivering the Gift. *Refine.*
*   [ ] **Draft Basic Privacy Snippet (Use AI Prompt ID3 cautiously):** Create initial text regarding data use. ***IMPORTANT: Obtain legal review for compliance.***
*   [ ] **Build Capture Form/Flow:** Implement the chosen mechanism using your website/landing page/chatbot tools.
*   [ ] **Configure ESP/CRM Integration:** Ensure captured leads (+tags) automatically flow into your system (e.g., set up Zapier or native integration).
*   [ ] **Set Up Basic Tagging:** Create the tag(s) defined in your strategy (Identify Grid B3) in your ESP/CRM.
*   [ ] **Implement Tracking Pixels:** Set up Meta Pixel / Google Tag events to fire on successful lead capture/form submission.
*   [ ] **Add Privacy Links/Consent:** Ensure necessary links to privacy policy and consent checkboxes (if required by regulations) are present.
*   [ ] **Test End-to-End Flow:** Submit a test lead. Verify: Form works -> Data appears in ESP/CRM -> Correct tag applied -> Delivery email received -> Tracking pixel fires.
*   [ ] **Output Check:** Confirm you have a working, integrated lead capture system.

#### **8. Implementation Guidance ("Now What?")**
*   **Format:** Text, Optional Short Video

You've strategized and drafted the components for IDENTIFY. Now, implement the tech:

1.  **Build the Form/Mechanism:** Use your website builder (e.g., Shopify theme sections, WordPress forms plugin), landing page tool (`Leadpages`, `Instapage`), or chatbot platform (`ManyChat`, `Tidio`) to create the actual opt-in interface based on your design from Identify Grid B1/C1 and using your refined "Ask" Copy (from Prompt ID1).
2.  **Connect to ESP/CRM:** This is vital. Configure the integration so when someone submits the form/provides info in chat:
    *   Their contact info (email, name) is added to the correct list/audience in your `Mailchimp`, `Klaviyo`, `ActiveCampaign`, etc.
    *   The specific Tag defined in Identify Grid B3 (e.g., `gift_checklist_download_apr25`) is automatically applied. (*How*: Often done via hidden fields, Zapier/Make connections, or native integrations).
3.  **Set Up Automation:** In your ESP/CRM, create an automation triggered *by that specific tag being applied*. The first step in this automation should immediately send the Welcome/Delivery Email containing the Gift link (using copy from Prompt ID2).
4.  **Implement Tracking:** Go to your `Meta Ads Manager` / `Google Analytics` / `Google Tag Manager`. Ensure your base pixels are installed correctly on the Gift landing page AND the thank-you page (or trigger confirmation event). Set up a "Lead" conversion event to fire specifically when the opt-in form is successfully submitted (often on the thank-you page load or via a button click trigger).
5.  **Add Compliance:** Ensure any legally required consent language/checkboxes are on your form, and link clearly to your Privacy Policy page.
6.  **TEST, TEST, TEST:** Go through the entire flow as if you were a user. Submit the form -> Check if you're added to the ESP list with the correct tag -> Check if you received the correct delivery email immediately -> Check if the conversion event registered in your tracking platforms. Debug any broken steps.
