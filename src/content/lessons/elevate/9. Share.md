#### **8. Implementation Guidance ("Now What?")**
*   **Format:** Text, Optional Short Video

You've planned how to activate your advocates. Let's deploy the systems:

1.  **Configure Review Requests:** Set up automated emails in your ESP or review platform (Judge.me, Yotpo, etc.) triggered by order fulfillment + delay (e.g., 14 days post-delivery). Use your refined request copy (from Prompt SH1).

5.  **Engage with Sharers:** Thank customers publicly/privately for reviews and referrals. Show appreciation to build goodwill.
6.  **Monitor SHARE Metrics:** Track review volume/rating, referral conversion rate, and qualitatively assess the impact of social proof on overall conversion rates.
