**(1. Intro | Module Introduction)**

*   **Format:** Text
*   **Content:**
    Welcome to Module 2, Step 2 of the Elevate Ecommerce Framework: **GIFT**. In Module 1 (HOOK), you successfully captured the attention of your ideal prospects on your chosen channels. But attention is incredibly fleeting. To convert that initial spark into genuine interest and build the foundation for a relationship, you need to immediately offer *tangible value*.
    The **objective of the GIFT step** is to solidify that captured attention by delivering a high-value, easily consumable resource (your lead magnet or "Gift") that solves a *specific*, often urgent, problem identified in their Foundation profile. This isn't just about getting an email address; it's a critical value exchange (core to Intent-Based Branding) that builds goodwill, demonstrates your expertise *before* asking for a sale, and perfectly positions you as a trusted guide. A well-designed GIFT motivates prospects to willingly move to the next step: **IDENTIFY**. Let's craft an irresistible offer.

**(8. Implementation | Implementation Guidance ("Now What?"))**

*   **Format:** Text, Optional Short Video
*   **Content:**
    You've designed and drafted your high-value GIFT and its supporting copy. Time to bring it to life:
    *   **Build the Landing Page:** Use your website platform (Shopify Page, WordPress) or dedicated tools (Leadpages, Instapage, ClickFunnels) to create the simple opt-in page using the copy generated and refined via Prompt G3. Keep it focused SOLELY on the Gift.
    *   **Set Up Email Delivery:** Configure your Email Service Provider (ESP - Mailchimp, Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign etc.) to automatically send the Gift Delivery Email (drafted via Prompt G5) immediately upon opt-in via the landing page form. Ensure the Gift link/attachment works.
    *   **Connect Hook to Gift:** Update your HOOK assets (Ads, Content CTAs, Social Posts) to direct traffic to your *new* GIFT Landing Page URL.
    *   **Integrate Promotional Copy:** Add the Ad Body Copy (Act 2) drafted via Prompt G4 to your ad campaigns, following the initial HOOK elements. Use the social post drafts to promote the Gift organically.
    *   **Initial Monitoring:** Start driving traffic and watch your Landing Page Opt-in Rate. Is the Gift resonating?
