Foundation Sub-Prompts (Categorized)
This is a list of granular prompts designed to help you build, refine, and deepen each pillar of your Foundation Blueprint. The prompts are categorized by their primary function: Strategic Analysis, Content Generation, and Customer Insight.

1. Strategic & Analytical Prompts
Use these prompts to analyze your research, define your market position, and make high-level strategic decisions.

Prompt FC-6: Brand Archetype Identification

Purpose: To define the core personality of your brand for consistent messaging.

Instruction: "Based on my Brand Story [Story Snippet], my Values [Value 1, Value 2], and my desired feeling of [Feeling], which of the 12 classic brand archetypes (e.g., The Sage, The Hero, The Outlaw) do I most align with? Provide your top 2 recommendations and explain why."

Prompt FM-1: Competitor Weakness Analysis

Purpose: To identify strategic opportunities by analyzing competitor failings.

Instruction: "Here is a set of negative reviews for my main competitor, [Competitor Name]: [Paste 3-5 negative reviews]. Analyze these to identify the top 3 recurring themes of customer dissatisfaction or product weakness."

Prompt FM-3: Market Trend Impact Analysis

Purpose: To understand how a broader trend affects your specific niche.

Instruction: "A major trend in my market is [Market Trend, e.g., 'the rise of AI in marketing']. How could this trend positively and negatively impact a business selling [My Product Category] to [Customer Avatar]? List 3 opportunities and 3 threats."

Prompt FM-4: Channel Communication Style Analysis

Purpose: To define how to communicate effectively on a specific platform.

Instruction: "Analyze the communication style of [Specific 'Watering Hole', e.g., a specific subreddit or Instagram hashtag]. What is the general tone (e.g., cynical, enthusiastic, technical)? What content formats get the most engagement? How should I adapt my [Brand Voice Adjectives] brand voice to be authentic here?"

Prompt FM-5: Blue Ocean Strategy - Underserved Niches

Purpose: To identify untapped market segments that competitors are ignoring.

Instruction: "My main competitors [Competitor 1, Competitor 2] appear to target [Broad Audience]. Based on my product's capabilities ([Unique Mechanism]), what are 3 potential underserved niche audiences within this broader market that they are likely overlooking?"

Prompt FCus-5: "Watering Hole" Discovery

Purpose: To brainstorm specific online locations where your avatar gathers.

Instruction: "My Customer Avatar is a [Role, e.g., 'Shopify store owner'] interested in [Topic, e.g., 'sustainable packaging']. Suggest 10 specific online 'watering holes' where they might hang out (e.g., specific subreddits, Facebook groups, influencers, blogs, or YouTube channels)."

Prompt FCus-6: Customer's Consideration Set

Purpose: To understand what alternatives (including non-direct competitors) the customer considers.

Instruction: "When my [Customer Avatar] decides to solve the PAIN of [Pain], what are the 4 types of solutions they likely consider? Think beyond direct competitors (e.g., 1. Do it themselves (DIY), 2. Hire a freelancer, 3. Use a different type of software, 4. Buy from a direct competitor like [Competitor Name])."

Step 1: STOP - Sub-Prompts
This is a list of granular prompts to help you strategize and create compelling STOP assets (scroll-stopping hooks). Use these after establishing your Foundation to generate the specific headlines, angles, and creative concepts needed to capture your ideal customer's attention.

The prompts are categorized by function: Strategic & Analytical, Content & Copywriting, and Creative & Visual.

1. Strategic & Analytical Prompts
Use these to define your angle of attack and plan for optimization.

Prompt ST-S1: Hook Angle & Emotion Selection

Purpose: To strategically decide what kind of hook to use before writing.

Instruction: "Analyze my Customer Avatar's core PAIN of [Pain] and primary GOAL of [Goal]. For a [Target Channel, e.g., Facebook Ad] campaign, recommend whether I should lead with a Pain-focused, Benefit-focused, or Intrigue-based hook. Justify your choice and suggest the primary emotion (Fear, Desire, or Curiosity) to trigger."

Prompt ST-S2: Competitor Hook Analysis

Purpose: To deconstruct competitor messaging to find strategic openings.

Instruction: "Here is the ad copy from my main competitor, [Competitor Name]: [Paste 1-2 examples of their ad copy]. Analyze their hook. What is their angle, what emotion do they target, and who do they seem to be speaking to? Identify one potential weakness or overused angle that I can counter-position against."

Prompt ST-S3: A/B Testing Plan Outline

Purpose: To create a structured plan for testing and optimizing your hooks.

Instruction: "I have three hook variations I want to test: [Hook A, Hook B, Hook C]. Outline a simple A/B testing plan for a [Facebook Ads] campaign. Define the primary KPI to measure success (e.g., CTR, Hold Rate), suggest a minimum daily budget, and a recommended test duration to determine a winner."

2. Content & Copywriting Prompts
Use these to generate the actual text for your hooks.

Prompt ST-C1: Pain-Agitation Hooks

Purpose: To create powerful hooks that resonate deeply with a known customer problem.

Instruction: "My customer's biggest PAIN is [Specific Pain, e.g., 'wasting hours on manual data entry']. Generate 5 scroll-stopping hooks for a [LinkedIn post] that agitate this specific pain and create a strong desire for a solution."

Prompt ST-C2: Dream Outcome Hooks

Purpose: To create aspirational hooks that focus on the ultimate benefit.

Instruction: "My customer's Dream Outcome is [Dream Outcome, e.g., 'to run their e-commerce store from a beach']. Generate 5 aspirational and benefit-driven hooks for an [Instagram Reel] that paints a vivid picture of this future state."

Prompt ST-C3: Intrigue & Curiosity Hooks

Purpose: To generate hooks with a high click-through rate by creating a knowledge gap.

Instruction: "My Unique Mechanism is [Mechanism, e.g., 'using AI to predict inventory needs']. Create 5 hooks that generate intense curiosity about this mechanism for a [Blog Post Title]. Use phrasing like 'The one mistake...' or 'Why 99% of people fail at...' without revealing the full solution."

Prompt ST-C4: Hook Adaptation for Multiple Channels

Purpose: To efficiently repurpose a winning message for different platforms.

Instruction: "My best performing Facebook ad hook is ['Hook text']. Adapt its core message for three other platforms: a professional LinkedIn article title, a concise Twitter/X post, and a keyword-rich Google Ads headline."

Prompt ST-C5: "Act 2" Bridge Copy

Purpose: To create the text that smoothly connects your hook to your lead magnet.

Instruction: "My STOP hook is ['Hook text']. The next step is to offer my GIFT, which is a ['Gift title and its key benefit']. Write a short bridge paragraph (2-3 sentences) for a Facebook ad that logically and persuasively transitions from the hook to the call-to-action for the Gift."

3. Creative & Visual Prompts
Use these to develop the visual components that support your text hooks.

Prompt ST-V1: Visual Hook Brainstorm

Purpose: To conceptualize the critical first 3 seconds of a video or a static ad image.

Instruction: "My text hook is ['Hook text']. Brainstorm 3 distinct visual concepts for an [Instagram Story Ad] that would visually stop the scroll and amplify the hook's message. For each concept, describe the opening shot or the static image layout."

Prompt ST-V2: Headline & Thumbnail Combination

Purpose: To create a powerful, unified message for video platforms or blogs.

Instruction: "My video (or blog post) is about [Topic]. Generate 5 compelling combinations of a YouTube Title (the hook) and a Thumbnail Description (the visual element) that work together to create maximum curiosity and a high click-through rate."

Instruction: "My competitor [Competitor Name] offers a [Competitor's Gift, e.g., 'Free Ebook on SEO'] on their website. Analyze its likely strengths and weaknesses. What is one way I can make my Gift on the same topic more valuable, actionable, or easier to consume?"

Prompt G-C1: Gift Title Variations

Purpose: To create compelling, benefit-driven titles for your lead magnet.

Prompt G-C2: Landing Page Copy (Headline & Bullets)

Purpose: To write persuasive copy for your Gift's opt-in page.

Instruction: "For my [Gift Title] landing page, write 3 powerful headline options and 5 benefit-driven bullet points. The copy should focus on the immediate value and transformation the user will experience after getting the Gift."

Prompt G-C3: Gift Content Outline
