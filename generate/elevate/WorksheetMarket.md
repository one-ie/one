## Market Sophistication Level (Target Audience Awareness)

***Instructions:*** *Based on Eugene Schwartz's framework (popularized by Kern/Brunson), how aware is your ideal customer (detailed in the upcoming 'People' section) of solutions like your Focus Product? Choose one level that best represents the *majority* of your target audience for your primary marketing.*

1.  **Select ONE Sophistication Level:**
    *   [ ] **Level 1: Completely Unaware:** They don't even know they have the problem yet. *(Marketing focuses on naming the pain).*
    *   [ ] **Level 2: Problem Aware:** They know they have the problem, but don't know solutions exist. *(Marketing agitates the pain, introduces the idea of solutions).*
    *   [ ] **Level 3: Solution Aware:** They know solutions exist, but don't know about *your* product or how it's different. *(Marketing introduces your Unique Mechanism/category of solution).*
    *   [ ] **Level 4: Product Aware:** They know about your product, but aren't sure if it's right for *them* or better than alternatives. *(Marketing highlights differentiation, offer stack, proof).*
    *   [ ] **Level 5: Most Aware:** They know your product and likely competitors; they just need the right deal/reason to buy *now*. *(Marketing focuses on the offer, urgency, scarcity, guarantees).*
