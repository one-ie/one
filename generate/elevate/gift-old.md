**Step 2: GIFT – Delivering Value, Capturing Interest**

You’ve successfully executed the **HOOK** – you've crafted a compelling message, chosen your channels, and captured the initial attention of your ideal customer. But attention is fleeting. Now, it's time to immediately solidify that interest by offering tangible value with Step 2: **GIFT**.

The GIFT step is fundamentally about **value exchange**. You provide a solution to a specific, urgent pain point your ideal customer is experiencing (identified in the Foundation stage) in the form of a high-value lead magnet. In return, they take the next step in expressing interest, preparing them for the IDENTIFY stage.

**Objective of the GIFT Step:**

The core goal is to **convert initial attention into genuine interest** by delivering a highly valuable, easily consumable resource (the "Gift") that solves a specific problem for your Avatar, motivating them to engage further and willingly provide their contact information later.

**Key Activities in This Step:**

1. **Refine & Develop the Lead Magnet:** Transform the offer concept from the HOOK step into a specific, tangible lead magnet (e.g., a checklist, template, short guide, cheat sheet, webinar registration, calculator, quiz). Ensure it directly addresses a key pain point from the Foundation and delivers a quick win.
    
2. **Create Lead Magnet Content:** Actually write, design, or record the content for the lead magnet itself. Keep it focused and high-value.
    
3. **Develop the Offer Presentation (Landing Page):** Create the simple landing page or opt-in mechanism where customers will claim the Gift. This page needs clear copy explaining the Gift's value and benefit.
    
4. **Craft Promotional Content (Ad Body - Act 2):** Write the supporting ad copy (the 'Meat' or Act 2), social media posts, or email content that introduces the Gift, explains its benefits clearly, and bridges the gap between the initial HOOK and the call to action to claim the Gift.
    
5. **Deploy Traffic Generation:** Execute the plan to drive traffic (using the channels selected in HOOK – e.g., running the ads with the copy developed) specifically to the Gift offer landing page.

- Your Customer Avatar profile & understanding of their specific pains (from Foundation).

    - **Chat Flow:** Asks for his primary ad platform, average ROAS/CPA goal, and one metric he's struggling with (e.g., high CTR but no conversions).
