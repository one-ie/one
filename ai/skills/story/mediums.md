# Mediums

**The beats never change between media. The order they are spoken in does.**

A story is told once. A page, a voice track, a short film, a thread and a pitch
are the same seven beats in five different orders. None of them is a re-telling,
and none of them may add a beat the teller did not give.

When a medium has no renderer, say so. Never fake it.

## Page

**Order:** Turn as the headline (the promise) · Knock · Want · Way as proof ·
Turn again as the payoff · one door.

- The **headline is a promise** and the **proof block is its payoff**. If they
  are not the same promise, the page is broken however good it reads.
- World and Cast are the subhead's job, in one line, or they are cut.
- Static-first: the story is words. If it needs JavaScript to be read, it is not
  a story yet.
- **One door at the end**, never three. On a story page the door is *tell yours*.

## Voice

**Order:** cold open on the Knock · World · Cast · Want · Way · Turn · Lesson.

- **A concrete scene before any explanation.** Serial's finding: the cold open
  earns the next ten minutes.
- Short sentences, specific detail. Radio drama works with no picture because
  the sentences are short and the detail is concrete.
- One voice. No music under the Knock.
- Never read a page aloud — the page's order is wrong for the ear.

## Video

**Order:** Turn in the first five seconds · Knock · Want · Way · Turn in full ·
Lesson.

- Short video learned in five seconds what podcasting learned in thirty. The
  payoff goes first, and the story explains it.
- Every beat must survive with the sound off.
- If the Turn is not visible, it is not a video — render it as a page.

## Conversation

**Order:** the spine, seven sentences, then one question.

- This is the default and the reflection shape. See `SKILL.md` § the loop.
- The teller offers, the machine reflects with one thing added, the teller
  confirms or corrects, the beat is filed. Seven beats is seven turns at most,
  often three.
- **Silence during the Way is correct.** Report settles, never effort.

## Thread

**Order:** Knock as post one · Want · Way as the middle · Turn · Lesson · door.

- One beat per post. A post carrying two beats loses both.
- Post one must stand alone and be worth reading if nobody reads post two.

## Testimonial

**Order:** who I was · what went wrong · what happened · what changed.

- This is Labov in one paragraph, and it is the shortest complete story form
  there is.
- It is the teller's words or it is worthless. Never write one.

## Pitch

**Order:** the shift in the world · winners and losers · the promised land ·
the gifts that get you there · the evidence.

- Strategic narrative, book register. The market is the hero.
- Ends on evidence, never on a request.

## Rules for every medium

1. **No beat is added.** A renderer that needs a beat the teller did not give
   asks for it, or renders without it.
2. **The Turn that is paid off is the Turn that was promised.** Every medium,
   no exception.
3. **The register is chosen once**, in the story, and every medium inherits it.
   A book-register story does not become commercial because it is now a video.
4. **A medium with no renderer is named, not faked.**
