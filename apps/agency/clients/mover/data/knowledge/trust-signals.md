# Trust Signals - Mover

## Certifications / Licenses (the hierarchy)

- **USDOT Number** - federal. Must be displayed on interstate carrier sites + trucks. Clickable to FMCSA SAFER lookup is a power move.
- **MC Number (MC-HHG)** - federal. Interstate household goods operating authority.
- **State intrastate license** - varies by state (CA Cal-T, FL IM, TX TxDMV, NY T-number, IL ICC MC, NJ PM, MA MDPU, WA UTC HG, OR ODOT, etc.). Must be visible on intrastate-only mover sites.
- **DOD ProMover program participant** - for military / GHC / TSP moves. [VERIFY for agency clients doing DOD work]
- **NASMM** (National Association of Senior Move Managers) - the cert for senior-move specialists. A-CSM / C-SMM designation.

## Industry certifications (THE big trust signals)

- **ProMover** (American Trucking Associations Moving & Storage Conference, formerly AMSA) - the top industry cert. Criminal background checks, consumer protection standards, ATA membership.
- **ATA Moving & Storage Conference** membership (the association that absorbed AMSA)
- **IAM (International Association of Movers)** - international / military moves
- **NASMM** - senior moves
- **Canadian Association of Movers (CAM)** - Canadian operators
- **BAR (British Association of Removers)** - UK (if relevant)

## Common review platforms (weighted by mover buyer importance)

- **Google Business Profile** - single most important. 4.5+ stars with 100+ reviews is the working threshold. Every Elite Movers / Bobcat / Maestro case shows this.
- **Yelp** - matters more in moving than in roofing. Negative Yelp reviews surface in Google search. Respond to all.
- **Facebook reviews** - especially for neighborhood / apartment complex referral flow
- **BBB** - customers cross-check here specifically because of scam concerns
- **Moving.com** reviews

## Insurance / bonding language

- "Licensed, bonded, and insured" - mandatory inclusion. Must specify:
  - **License:** USDOT + MC (interstate) and/or state intrastate license number
  - **Bond:** cargo bond (federal - $75,000 minimum for household goods brokers; carriers have cargo insurance not bond)
  - **Insurance:** general liability ($1M typical), cargo insurance (FMCSA minimum $10,000 per vehicle / $5,000 per claim for household goods), workers comp (statutory by state), commercial auto
- "Full Value Protection offered" - FMCSA-specific term. If offered, say so; rate is typically $6-$12 per $1,000 of declared value.
- "Released value included" - the $0.60/lb federal default. State it plainly, offer FVP upsell.
- "COI (Certificate of Insurance) available within 24-48 hours" - big for apartment + commercial

## What does NOT impress this buyer

- Stock photos of trucks / crews labeled "our team" - customers check the GMB photos and can tell
- "Family owned" with no named owner / photo
- "Over X years of experience" with no founding year
- Generic "we care about your belongings" filler
- "We're insured" with no specifics (which coverage, what limit)
- Obscure association badges with no recognition value
- "Voted #1" without source + year
- Testimonials with first-name-only and no city / move-type context
- "We move anywhere" - sounds unserious / scammy

## Power proof points (use prominently)

- **USDOT [number] / MC [number]** displayed in footer (clickable to SAFER)
- **State license #** displayed for intrastate-only movers
- **"ProMover Certified"** badge (if real)
- **"[Number]+ moves completed since [year]"** with real count
- **"[Number] Google reviews / [X.X] star average"** with live embed
- **Real fleet photo** (branded trucks)
- **Real crew photo** (not stock) with named team members
- **Owner photo + bio** ("[Owner] moved their first family in [year]...")
- **Before / after or during-move photos** from real jobs
- **Named apartment complexes served** ("We have COI on file for 40+ Ventura / Orlando / San Marcos apartment buildings")
- **Binding / not-to-exceed estimate offered in writing** (addresses hostage-loading fear)
- **"No deposit required for standard local moves"** (if true)
- **In-home or video estimate available** - biggest conversion lever
- **Response time commitment** ("We respond to every quote within 1 business hour")

## Red-flag competitors to differentiate from

- Broker-only operations (no USDOT-authorized MC, subcontract the move)
- Cash-only operators
- "FREE moving quote" sites with no named business, just a form
- Sites with no USDOT / MC / state license visible
- Sites advertising "lowest price guaranteed"
- Sites with no owner / no crew photos / stock everything
