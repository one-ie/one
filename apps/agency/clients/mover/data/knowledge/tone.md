# Tone Pack - Mover

Real moving trade language. Signals you've actually done the work.

- local move / long-distance move / interstate / intrastate
- hourly rate / 2-hour minimum / 4-hour minimum
- binding estimate / non-binding estimate / binding not-to-exceed
- in-home estimate / video estimate / virtual walkthrough
- Bill of Lading / inventory sheet / high-value inventory form
- Order for Service
- pickup window / delivery window / delivery spread
- released value / Full Value Protection (FVP)
- USDOT / MC number / MC-HHG / SAFER
- long carry / stair carry / elevator reservation
- shuttle fee / long-carry fee / flight charge / bulky-item fee
- crating / custom crate / specialty crating
- moving blankets / pads / shrink wrap / stretch wrap
- wardrobe box / dish pack / picture box / mattress bag / TV box
- disassembly / reassembly
- pack / unpack / full-pack / fragile-pack
- tape, paper, packing peanuts
- piano board / piano dolly / 4-wheel dolly / appliance dolly
- hand truck / two-wheeler
- furniture pad / loading ramp / walk board
- air ride suspension / tractor-trailer / box truck / 26-footer / cube van
- storage-in-transit (SIT) / crated storage / vault storage / warehouse storage
- apartment COI / certificate of insurance / additional insured
- ProMover / AMSA (legacy term) / ATA Moving & Storage Conference
- same-day move / next-day availability
- NASMM (senior moves)

## Vocabulary - DON'T use

Outsider tells — scream "marketer wrote this".

- "moving solutions"
- "premier moving experience"
- "we leverage our fleet"
- "your trusted moving partner"
- "elevate your move"
- "seamless transition to your new home"
- "synergy between packing and transport" (yes, really, this gets written)
- "transform your moving journey"
- "world-class relocation"
- "passionate about helping families move"
- "cutting-edge moving technology"
- "holistic moving approach"
- "optimize your move"

## Tone notes

- **Formality level:** casual-professional. Talks like a person, not a brochure.
- **Urgency level:** medium (book-ahead buyer) to high (last-minute / same-day).
- **Authority signals:** USDOT + MC, state license, ProMover cert, BBB accreditation, real review count, named owner, years in business, # of moves done, named apartment complexes served.
- **Emotional register:** reassuring, no-BS, empathetic. "We know moving sucks" is the starting emotional posture. Then: "here's how we make it less bad."
- **Regional voice:** sound like a mover from that region, not a Madison-Ave copywriter. Texan mover talks like a Texan. NY mover talks fast and direct. CA mover can be a bit warmer.
- **Transparency posture:** disclose pricing variability openly. Customers who've been scammed respect the mover who says "here's why quotes vary" instead of pretending they don't.

## Sample headline

GOOD (movers-who-move voice):
> **Honest movers for [City]. No hostage loading. No surprise fees. Book a video estimate in 60 seconds.**

GOOD (reassuring, local):
> **We've moved over 3,200 [City] families since 2014. Here's how we keep your stuff safe — and your quote honest.**

BAD (marketer voice - do NOT write this):
> *Premier relocation solutions for the discerning [City] family. Leverage our world-class moving experience for a seamless transition.*

## Sample paragraph

GOOD:
> Moving is stressful enough without wondering if your mover is going to double the quote halfway through the day. Every [Company] move starts with a written binding estimate — the price we quote is the price you pay. Your USDOT and MC numbers are in our footer so you can verify us on FMCSA SAFER in ten seconds. Our crew is named on your Bill of Lading (no day labor), and we've had the same three trucks on the road since 2019. Call [phone] or book a 15-minute video walkthrough — we'll give you a real number, not a range.

BAD:
> *At [Company], we understand that moving is more than just a change of address — it's a transformative journey. Our world-class team of relocation specialists is passionate about delivering seamless moving solutions tailored to your unique needs. Trust us to elevate your moving experience to the next level.*

## Avoid (niche-specific)

- "FREE move" / "FREE first hour" in caps (spam trigger per Rule 07 + legal exposure)
- "Guaranteed pickup date" (FMCSA-sensitive — movers give windows, not dates)
- "Lowest price guaranteed" (bait-and-switch scammer language)
- "No hidden fees" without a published fee list (empty claim)
- "100% damage-free" (unsustainable contractual claim)
- "We move anywhere" (sounds unserious)
- Fear-mongering about competing movers (stay factual about red flags, don't name-call)
- "Licensed, bonded, and insured" without specifying what (say the USDOT / MC / state license number)
- Any "guarantee" language without a defined remedy
- "Leverage" / "synergy" / "optimize" / "strategy" (consulting-speak per agency Rule 06)
- Stock photos of European movers on a US mover site
- "As seen on [TV show]" without evidence
- Using "AMSA certified" (outdated - it's ProMover now)

## Regional flavor cues

- **CA coastal (Ventura, SB, LA, SF):** premium / luxury angle works. White-glove. "We moved the Goldsteins into Montecito" type proof. Cal-T number front and center.
- **FL (Orlando, Tampa, Miami):** retiree-heavy + relocation-heavy. "Moving to Florida" search volume. Apartment complex play is strong in Orlando metro.
- **TX (DFW, Austin, Houston, San Marcos):** volume play. Price-sensitive + long-distance (TX to everywhere). "Texan-friendly" plain voice works.
- **NYC / NJ:** walk-up / elevator / COI gatekeeping drives everything. Hourly rate + minimum transparency is essential. T-number + NYC DCWP on every page.
- **PNW (Portland / Seattle / Vancouver):** environmental angle (reusable bins, donation haul-away) works. UTC / ODOT visible.
- **Canada (Vancouver / Toronto):** CAM membership is the trust marker. Cross-border to US = separate license regime.
