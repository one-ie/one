# Shops made with ONE

Every shop below takes money into its owner's own accounts. Add yours with a
pull request: one line, newest at the top.

Each shop serves a signed statement at `/.well-known/one.json`. Anyone can
check it: the signature verifies against the `publicKey` in it, and that key is
the shop's own Sui key.
