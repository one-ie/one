## Product Details

The Classic Canvas Backpack combines heritage craftsmanship with modern functionality. Constructed from heavy-duty waxed canvas and premium leather, it's designed to handle daily commutes and weekend adventures with equal ease.

### Features

- **Waxed Canvas Construction**: Naturally water-resistant and incredibly durable
- **Leather Accents**: Full-grain leather straps and reinforcements
- **Padded Laptop Sleeve**: Fits up to 15" laptop with protective cushioning
- **Organized Storage**: Multiple interior and exterior pockets
- **Comfortable Carry**: Padded shoulder straps and reinforced back panel
- **Roll-Top Closure**: Expandable capacity with secure buckle system
- **YKK Zippers**: Premium hardware built to last

### Specifications

- **Dimensions**: 18" (H) x 12" (W) x 6" (D)
- **Capacity**: 22 liters (expandable to 28L with roll-top)
- **Weight**: 2.8 lbs
- **Laptop Compartment**: Fits up to 15" laptop
- **Water Bottle Pockets**: External side pockets
- **Front Organizer**: Quick-access pocket with pen slots and key clip

### Available Colors

- **Olive**: Classic military-inspired green
- **Navy**: Deep maritime blue
- **Charcoal**: Versatile dark gray

### Care Instructions

Waxed canvas is designed to develop character over time:
- Spot clean with cold water and soft cloth
- For deeper cleaning, use canvas cleaner
- Re-wax annually with wax bar (included)
- Leather will darken and patina with use
- Avoid machine washing

### Thoughtful Design

Every detail is considered:
- **Padded laptop sleeve** with fleece lining protects your tech
- **Quick-access front pocket** for items you need frequently
- **Water bottle pockets** keep hydration within reach
- **Internal organization** with zippered pockets and slip compartments
- **Leather handle** for grab-and-go convenience
- **D-rings** for attaching accessories

### Built to Last

This isn't a disposable backpack. We use materials and construction techniques designed for decades of use:
- 18 oz waxed canvas resists abrasion and weather
- Triple-stitched stress points prevent failure
- Brass hardware won't rust or corrode
- Full-grain leather accents develop beautiful patina
- Reinforced bottom panel handles heavy loads

### Heritage Craftsmanship

Made in Scotland by a workshop that's been crafting canvas goods for over 100 years. Traditional techniques meet modern design for a backpack that honors the past while serving the present.

### Water Resistance

The waxed canvas coating provides excellent water resistance for everyday use. While not fully waterproofed, it will keep contents dry in rain and light weather. The included wax bar allows you to maintain water resistance over time.
