### About Giclée Printing

Giclée (pronounced "zhee-clay") is a fine art printing process that uses archival inks and museum-grade paper to create prints virtually identical to original artworks. Benefits include:

- Microscopic ink droplets create smooth tonal transitions
- 12-color process achieves broader color gamut than traditional printing
- Pigment-based inks resist fading for 100+ years
- Archival paper prevents yellowing and degradation

### Care & Preservation

To maintain print quality for generations:

- Frame behind UV-protective glass or acrylic
- Avoid direct sunlight and bright artificial light
- Display in climate-controlled environments
- Handle edges only, avoid touching print surface
- Store flat in acid-free portfolio if unframed
