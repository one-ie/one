### Candle Care

For best performance and safety:

**First Burn (Important!):**

- Burn for 3-4 hours until wax pool reaches edges
- This prevents tunneling and ensures even burn

**Every Use:**

- Trim wick to 1/4" before lighting
- Burn on heat-resistant surface away from drafts
- Never burn for more than 4 hours at a time
- Keep away from flammable materials
- Never leave burning candle unattended
- Discontinue use when 1/2" of wax remains

### Why Soy Wax?

Soy wax offers significant advantages over traditional paraffin:

- **Clean Burn**: Produces minimal soot
- **Renewable**: Made from USA-grown soybeans
- **Biodegradable**: Breaks down naturally
- **Better Scent Throw**: Holds and releases fragrance evenly
- **Longer Burn**: Burns 30-50% longer than paraffin
- **Easy Cleanup**: Spills clean with soap and water
