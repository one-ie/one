### Care Instructions

Linen is a natural fiber that becomes softer with each wash. Machine wash cold with like colors, line dry for best results. Iron while slightly damp using medium heat for a crisp look, or embrace the natural wrinkles for a relaxed vibe.

### Styling Tips

- Pair with sandals for casual daytime wear
- Dress up with heels and statement jewelry for evening
- Layer with a denim jacket for cooler evenings
- Belt at waist or leave flowing for different silhouettes

### Sustainability

Made from 100% organic linen grown in European flax fields without pesticides. Manufactured in Italy using traditional techniques that minimize water usage and environmental impact. Linen is one of the most sustainable fabrics - it's biodegradable, requires minimal processing, and gets better with age.

### About Linen

Linen is prized for its exceptional breathability, natural luster, and ability to keep you cool in warm weather. As a natural fiber, it will soften and develop character with wear, becoming a cherished piece in your wardrobe for years to come.
