## Product Details

Our Minimalist Leather Wallet is designed for those who appreciate quality craftsmanship and timeless design. Made from premium full-grain vegetable-tanned leather, it develops a rich patina over time, becoming more beautiful with age.

### Features

- **Full-Grain Leather**: The highest quality leather that ages beautifully
- **RFID Protection**: Built-in shielding protects against electronic theft
- **Slim Profile**: Fits comfortably in front or back pocket without bulk
- **Thoughtful Layout**: Organized storage for cards, cash, and coins
- **Vegetable Tanned**: Eco-friendly tanning process using natural materials
- **Hand-Stitched**: Reinforced stitching ensures decades of use

### Specifications

- **Dimensions**: 4.25" (L) x 3" (W) x 0.375" (D)
- **Weight**: 2.1 oz
- **Card Slots**: 6 dedicated slots
- **Bill Compartment**: Holds folded bills
- **Coin Pocket**: Secure snap closure

### Available Colors

- **Cognac**: Warm brown that develops honey tones
- **Black**: Classic black with gray undertones

### Care Instructions

Your wallet will develop character with use. For maintenance:
- Wipe with slightly damp cloth to remove dirt
- Apply leather conditioner every 3-6 months
- Avoid prolonged water exposure
- Store in cool, dry place when not in use

### The Patina Process

Full-grain leather is a living material. As you use your wallet, natural oils from your hands and exposure to elements create a unique patina - a rich, lustrous finish that tells your story. No two wallets will age the same way.

### Craftsmanship

Each wallet is handcrafted by skilled artisans in our workshop in Portland, Oregon. From cutting the leather to the final stitch, every step is done with care and precision. We use traditional leatherworking techniques that have been perfected over generations.

### Sustainability

Our leather is sourced from tanneries that use environmentally responsible vegetable tanning processes. This traditional method uses natural tannins from tree bark instead of harsh chemicals, resulting in a product that's better for you and the planet.

### Lifetime Guarantee

We stand behind our craftsmanship. This wallet is guaranteed against defects in materials and workmanship for life. If you ever have an issue, we'll repair or replace it.
