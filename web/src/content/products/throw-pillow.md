## Product Details

These Decorative Throw Pillows are the perfect finishing touch for any room. Featuring unique hand-printed designs on organic cotton and filled with premium recycled materials, they combine style with sustainability.

### Features

- **Organic Cotton Cover**: GOTS certified organic cotton, soft and breathable
- **Removable & Washable**: Hidden zipper allows easy cleaning
- **Eco-Friendly Fill**: 100% recycled polyester fiber insert
- **Hand-Printed Designs**: Unique patterns using water-based inks
- **Colorfast Dyes**: Won't fade or bleed in wash
- **Plush Comfort**: Generously filled for perfect support
- **Hidden Zipper**: Invisible closure maintains clean aesthetic

### Specifications

- **Size**: 18" x 18" (standard throw pillow size)
- **Cover Material**: 100% GOTS certified organic cotton
- **Fill Material**: 100% recycled polyester fiber
- **Fill Weight**: 18 oz (medium-firm support)
- **Thread Count**: 200
- **Weight**: 1.2 lbs (with insert)

### Available Patterns

- **Geometric**: Modern triangles and shapes in navy and cream
- **Botanical**: Delicate hand-drawn leaves and stems in sage green
- **Abstract**: Bold brushstrokes in terracotta and charcoal
- **Minimalist**: Clean lines and shapes in black and white

### Care Instructions

**Cover (Machine Washable):**
- Remove insert via hidden zipper
- Machine wash cold with like colors
- Tumble dry low or line dry
- Iron on low heat if needed (avoid printed areas)
- Do not bleach

**Insert:**
- Spot clean only
- Air dry completely before reinserting
- Fluff regularly to maintain shape

### Design Story

Each pillow features original artwork created by independent designers and hand-printed using traditional block printing techniques. The process:
1. Designs are carved into wooden blocks
2. Water-based inks are applied to blocks
3. Skilled artisans hand-print each cover
4. Slight variations ensure each piece is unique

### Sustainability

We're committed to sustainable home goods:
- **Organic Cotton**: Grown without pesticides or synthetic fertilizers
- **Recycled Fill**: Diverts plastic bottles from landfills
- **Water-Based Inks**: Non-toxic, low environmental impact
- **Fair Trade**: Made in Fair Trade certified workshops
- **Recyclable Packaging**: Ships in compostable materials

### Styling Tips

Mix and match patterns for dynamic looks:

- Layer 3-5 pillows in complementary patterns
- Use as accent pieces on sofas, beds, or chairs
- Rotate covers seasonally for fresh looks
