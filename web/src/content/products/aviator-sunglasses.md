### Why Polarized?

Polarized lenses block horizontally oriented light waves (glare) while allowing vertical waves through. This technology:
- Eliminates glare from water, roads, and reflective surfaces
- Reduces eye strain and fatigue
- Enhances color and contrast
- Improves visual clarity in bright conditions
- Perfect for driving, water sports, and outdoor activities

### Care & Maintenance

To keep your sunglasses in pristine condition:
- Clean lenses with included microfiber cloth
- Use lens cleaning solution for stubborn smudges
- Store in protective hard case when not in use
- Avoid leaving in extreme heat (car dashboard)
- Rinse with fresh water after salt water exposure
