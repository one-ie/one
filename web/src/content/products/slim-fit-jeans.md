## Product Details

Crafted from premium Japanese selvedge denim, these Slim Fit Jeans combine timeless style with modern comfort. The subtle stretch ensures all-day comfort without sacrificing the authentic denim look and feel.

### Features

- **Premium Selvedge Denim**: Woven on traditional shuttle looms in Japan
- **Slight Stretch**: 2% elastane provides flexibility and comfort
- **YKK Hardware**: Premium zipper and rivets built to last
- **Reinforced Construction**: Bar-tacked stress points for durability
- **Classic 5-Pocket**: Timeless design that never goes out of style
- **Mid Rise**: Sits comfortably at natural waist

### Sizing Guide

True to size with slim fit through hip and thigh, slight taper from knee to ankle.

- **28**: Waist 28", Inseam 32"
- **30**: Waist 30", Inseam 32"
- **32**: Waist 32", Inseam 32"
- **34**: Waist 34", Inseam 32"
- **36**: Waist 36", Inseam 32"

*All measurements are approximate. For inseam alterations, please consult a local tailor.*

### Available Washes

- **Indigo**: Classic blue denim with subtle fading
- **Black**: Deep black with minimal contrast stitching

### Care Instructions

For best results and to preserve color, wash inside out in cold water with like colors. Hang dry or tumble dry low. Avoid harsh detergents and bleach. Raw denim will fade beautifully over time with proper care.

### Break-In Period

Like all premium denim, these jeans will mold to your body over the first few wears. The fabric will soften while maintaining its structure, creating a personalized fit unique to you.

### Craftsmanship

Woven in Okayama, Japan - the denim capital of the world - on vintage shuttle looms that create the signature selvedge edge. Each pair represents generations of textile expertise.
